Turtle vs Rabbit

The Long Race.

How to play

You are the Turtle. The Rabbit is faster, but it tires. Be ahead of it when time runs out, or reach the finish line first.

Space / hold SprintBurns stamina fastShift / SteadySteady pace, cheap staminaA / D, arrows, W / SChange laneE / UseUse held power-upQ / Guard4s armor, 25s cooldownEsc or PPause

The Turtle has 50% more stamina than the Rabbit and two skills: Steady and Shell Guard. The Rabbit has Burst and a Dust Kick that slows you when it bursts past close by.

Dodge logs, rocks, mud and bees. Collect: 🥕 speed, 🍃 +25 stamina, 🐚 shield, ⏰ slows the Rabbit, 🍄 surprise. Swipe up or down on touch screens.
